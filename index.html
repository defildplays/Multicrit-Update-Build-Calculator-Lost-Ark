Why are the Crit Rates different?

Master adds 7% Crit Rate at full stacks. Keen Sense 2 adds 8%. The Blunt Thorn side uses both, so its total is 15 percentage points above the starting stats. The other side adds 7% when using Master, or 0% when using Pulverize. Standing Striker and Blunt Thorn themselves add no Crit Rate.

The row selector shows the final total for each side; you do not need to subtract anything. For Blunt Thorn, Crit Rate above 80% is converted into Evolution Damage. Its pre-conversion total still matters, even though its actual chance to Crit stays at 80%.

Crit Damage columns include the 200% base and applicable bonuses. Each cell is Blunt Thorn’s percentage damage difference relative to the other build, with the two Crit totals shown in that row. These are fixed node combinations, not optimized results for every possible character. Use the setup checker for your own nodes, speed, MP cost and damage pools.

How Unlimited Magick affects the result

Unlimited Magick reduces the cooldown of MP-consuming skills. It increases sustained DPS when that reduction produces useful extra casts. The checker keeps your selected Tier 3 node and reports damage per hit; it does not predict the DPS gained from changing your rotation.

Understand the change

Crit Rate above 100% has value

Without Blunt Thorn

Extra Crit Rate rolls another copy of the critical bonus. At 250% Crit Damage, that bonus is 150% of base damage. A Multi-Crit is 250 + 150 = 400%, rather than 250 × 2.

At 120% Crit Rate, an ordinary crit is 250%, a Multi-Crit is 400%, and the average is 280%. The same rule continues past 200% and 300% Crit Rate. Effects triggered “on Crit” still trigger once per hit.

With Blunt Thorn 2

Actual Crit Rate stays capped at 80%. Excess pre-cap Crit Rate becomes Evolution Damage: 19% + 1.4 × excess above 80%. The Evolution Damage ceiling is removed.

At 120% pre-cap Crit Rate: 19 + 1.4 × 40 = 75% Evolution Damage. At 130%, it becomes 89%. Blunt Thorn cannot Multi-Crit.

Use the right inputs

What can change your result?

Skill-specific Crit stats

Use the Crit Rate and Crit Damage active on your main damage skill. Include its Tripods, class buffs and Ark Grid effects. A bonus that applies to one skill does not apply to your whole rotation.

Buff and stack uptime

The calculation assumes full Master and Standing Striker stacks, and the entered buffs are active. Frequent stack losses or missing buffs reduce the benefit.

Separate damage pools

Additional Damage and Evolution Damage are separate pools. Enter the correct total for each: a bonus is less valuable when you already have more of the same pool.

Damage per hit vs DPS

The percentage gain describes expected damage per hit. Cooldown reduction, mana availability and useful extra casts also affect sustained DPS.

Supersonic Breakthrough and MP Furnace

Supersonic Breakthrough: enter Attack and Move Speed before their caps, including active buffs. Its full bonus depends on those values.

MP Furnace: enter the skill’s original MP cost before reductions. A non-MP skill receives no damage bonus from this node. At level 2, 480 base MP reaches its 24% Evolution Damage cap.

What the recommendation covers

The checker retains your Tier 3 choice and required Tier 2 cooldown or mana node group. It selects the remaining supported damage nodes and eligible Tier 5 option.

Speed or MP-dependent nodes are excluded until their required inputs are supplied. Guaranteed-Crit skills with their own Crit Rate conversion need separate handling. Enter extra damage on Crit under optional accuracy when your gear, core or party provides it. Close results can change with skill-specific effects and buff uptime.
